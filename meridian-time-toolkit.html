About Meridian

Meridian started as a clock studio and grew into a small toolkit for the actual problems people run into around time: knowing what time it is somewhere else, staying focused, finding a meeting slot that works for two cities, and waking up on time.

Every clock, timer, and grid on this site runs on your device's real clock — nothing simulated. Your world clock cities, focus history, alarms, and preferences are saved to this browser, so they're still here next time you open the site.
